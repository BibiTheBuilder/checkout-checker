# Checkout Checker

A simple browser-based tool to catch supermarket deals that didn't get applied at checkout.

**[Try it →](https://bibithebuilder.github.io/checkout-checker/)**

## How it works

1. Log the deals you found (enter the price directly, or enter the regular price and pick the deal type — 50% off, 2 for 1, etc.)
2. Snap a photo of your receipt (or several photos for long ones)
3. The tool reads the receipt in your browser and tells you which deals are missing or have the wrong price

Works on phone or desktop. Pick **Chill** mode for calm colors, or **CHAOS** mode for flying kittens with lasers.

## Privacy

- Everything runs in your browser — receipt photos never leave your device.
- No backend, no tracking, no accounts. Just open the page.

## Tech

One self-contained HTML file. OCR via [Tesseract.js](https://tesseract.projectnaptha.com/). Tap "Add to Home Screen" in Safari/Chrome to use it like an app.
